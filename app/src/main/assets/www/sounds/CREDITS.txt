Animal sound recordings used in First Game
==========================================

All recordings are public domain (CC0 1.0). They were trimmed, filtered,
denoised, loudness-matched and re-encoded to OGG Vorbis for the game.

  - Annoyed hen (ID 0453)           -> farm/hen_*.ogg
  - Hen lays #2 (ID 0976)           -> farm/henlay_*.ogg
  - Song of rooster (ID 0283)       -> farm/rooster_1.ogg
  - Cock song #1 (ID 0440)          -> farm/rooster_2.ogg

Freesound.org — downloaded with the CC0 license filter
  - "duck quacking" by digpro120 (sound 558858)          -> farm/duck_*.ogg

  - "turkey gobble 4 96khz" by jarredgibb (sound 233126)  -> farm/turkey_1.ogg

Train (sounds/train/) — BigSoundBank.com by Joseph Sardin, CC0 (public domain)

  - Train whistle, foley #7 (ID 3321)    -> whistle_2.ogg
  - Train whistle, foley #2 (ID 3316)    -> whistle_3.ogg
  Made with tools/sound/train_sounds.py (cut, high-pass, level-matched, loop points for held whistle).

All other sounds in the game are synthesized in code (Web Audio).
